# The Agastya Advanced Wheelchair

Idea curated in April 2026

Disclaimer: This system is designed to give reminders, help reduce pressure and moisture buildup that may contribute to skin irritation while sitting in a wheelchair. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical or clinical accuracy.

Wheelchair users who remain seated for long periods of time may experience discomfort and irritation due to pressure and moisture buildup. Caregivers may find it difficult to continuously remind the person to change their position at times. Many existing smart wheelchair solutions to these problems are expensive and inaccessible for everyday users. The Agastya ADVANCED Wheelchair is designed as a very affordable wheelchair that provides pressure reminders, cooling, and precautionary alerts.
